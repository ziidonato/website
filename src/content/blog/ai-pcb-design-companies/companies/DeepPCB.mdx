DeepPCB is actually a product of InstaDeep (visible in their footer), and
InstaDeep is owned by BioNTech, a pharmaceutical company that got started
selling early-warning tests for COVID-19, whose founders are stepping down.

## Products

### Auto-Router

It seems like that's the main product here. An engine to route your PCBs.
Benchmarks show boards that would take 7 days to route taking ~2 hours. They
support Altium, KiCad, EasyEDA, Zuken, Orcad, Eagle, and more.

### Cooper

An AI layout reviewer. It can analyze your board and explain decisions to you,
point out mistakes, etc. "Chat-with-a-PCB" inside your ECAD. I only see a KiCAD
plugin, but you can upload files to their web app, where you probably get more
features there.

## What they do well

Pricing. They have an actual pricing page, and it seems like they bill by
allocated time. You get a 30-minute free trial that supports up to 4 layer
boards, up to 100 components, and 2200 pins, which is an awful lot for most
boards. Then you pay for more allocated time, with an hour being $30, and
savings for bulk. Enterprise teams can contact them for things like on-premesis
deployments.

## What they suck at

I don't see anything about building the schematic. Honestly, I think that's
fine, since there are other startups in that field already. It still feels kinda
weird to segment them out like that, or to have one and not the other. Maybe if
you really want, you can combine a schematic generator with a router like this,
haha.

I also don't like the (probably) fake marketing.
[100,000 engineers, 18,000 boards](https://deeppcb.ai/cooper/)? really? I'm
young but I wasn't born yesterday.
