Based in Austin, TX, Protoflow LLC seems to be marketed toward hobbyists and
educational institutions, rather than corporations and serious builders. This is
definitely one of the smaller companies on this list, and while I won't dock any
points from them for that, buyers might have their own opinions.

## Products

### Protoflow

"Free PCB desing software with AI built in." This is its own ECAD. Their
[demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3M97ruhVrU) shows a chatbot on the
right side, generating schematics and PCBs that you can then view and edit in
the main viewer. You can also run spice simulations and view datasheets.
"Trusted by 20k+ engineers at Logitech, Honda, Rolls Royce, Samsung, and
others."

Looking at their pricing, the editor itself is free, with "5 basic + 10 pro AI
messages/day", and 10 "generation units"/month for AI part generation. For
$20/month, you can get 50 AI credits/day (250 per rolling week), Gemini Flash as
a "pro model", "simulation verification", and 30 "generation units"/month. There
are also Max and Ultra plans, and an enterprise option.

### PartSpark

They're actually running this completely separately from Protoflow. Same team,
the contact email is `admin@protoflow.ai`, but it's a
[different domain](https://partspark.app/) and they don't reference each other.
I'm probably messing up their strategy by linking them together like this but oh
well I'm probably messing up their SEO strategy by linking them together like
this but oh well.

PartSpark is a symbol/footprint generator. You can search for parts or upload a
PDF, and it'll generate the symbols/footprints for your ECAD. Free plan with 10
generation units, pro at \$15/month with 30 generation units, and other plans
which go up to \$60/month.

## What they do well

Public pricing page with plenty of options. Again, should really be a standard
thing, but when you have products without it, those that do have it stand out.
They also have at least something of a community, with a discord server that has
~800 members.

## What they suck at

I don't think engineers at Logitech, Honda, etc. are using this. This EDA is
designed as hobbyist-first, and I don't see any of the compliance and security
standards that large companies such as those would require. We really need to
stop with the fake marketing.

[The founders have posted on Reddit about their app](https://www.reddit.com/r/PCB/comments/1v2fnb1/been_building_a_new_pcb_design_tool/),
and were met with some of the same criticisms too, along with some othes,
including quality of the app.

Another problem is that ProtoFlow is the name of a prostate supplement, so
they're having a bit of a name clash there. Guess they're banking on winning the
SEO war with them, or something.
