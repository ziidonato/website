BQR Reliability Engineering was founded in 1989 in Israel as a consultancy, who
then started building software. They advertise 600+ customers managing 4,500+
projects, and name Altium, Digikey, and OrCAD as explicit partners, among other
major players in the industry. Advertised customers include NVidia, Intel,
Siemens, Cisco, ABB, Boeing, Lockheed Martin, among other serious customers.

## Products

### Synthelyzer

[Synthelyzer](https://www.bqr.com/products/ecad-plug-in) is an ECAD plugin that
works inside Altium Designer, OrCAD, or Siemens Xpedition. As you draw your
circuit, it runs a number of analyses and checks, and the findings appear on the
schametic for you to edit. Checks include:

- Part-stress analysis
- schematic rule checking
- MTBF prediction
- Thermal-aware analysis
- Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) and traceability

They advertise other benefits like cost optimization, reliability and
performance improvements, physical optimization, etc. There's no public pricing
since they give out custom quotes, so it's probably pricey.

### Circuit Hawk

Circuit Hawk is their circuit simulation software that runs
["rule-based checks, connectivity verification, and realistic stress estimation"](https://www.bqr.com/products/circuithawk).
This seems like the same thing as the ECAD plugin but with multi-board support.
I don't see any demos of the product so I can't say for sure.

### fiXtress

This seems like the Thermal analysis and MTBF prediction part of their ECAD
plugin. This one actually has
[something of a video](https://www.bqr.com/videos/fixtress/fixtress-20260922.mp4)
so we can see how it works.

### CARE

Their reliability, availability, maintainability and safety (RAMS) analysis.
They advertise support for a number of standards such as IEC61025 and
MIL-STD-1629. Features include:

- FMEA | FMECA (FME (Criticality) A) | RPN (Risk Priority Number)
- Fault Tree Analysis
- Fault Detection & simulation
- Mean Time to Repair

### apmOptimizer

This is their optimization branch. They advertise things like CAPEX/OPEX
breakdowns, Level of Repair Analysis, Scheduled maintenance optimation, spare
parts levels, and more.

## what they do well

Their suite gives them all the data needed to perform analyses and simulations
that other solutions wouldn't have the coherence for. The ECAD integration is
also a plus, meaning engineers don't have to leave the tools they're using.

## Concerns

There is no discussion about them at all online. No forum threads, independent
reviews, nothing. For a company that boasts about having so many high-level
clients, that is very strange to say the least. Maybe one could say "no news is
good news" but I don't know, it seems fishy.
