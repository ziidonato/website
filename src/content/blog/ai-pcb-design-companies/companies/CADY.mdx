CADY is another Israeli firm founded by 3 IDF veterans. Their sole product is
their schematic analysis tool, which helps users "ship hardware with AI."
Advertised clients include Amazon, Ford, Sandisk, Yamaha, and others.

## CADY (Product)

Their sole product, CADY, is a schematic analysis tool that takes a netlist and
BOM files from various ECAD appliations (their guide lists 8 possible
environments) and analyzes them using an AI agent. There is actually little
information here on how it works, but there is a lot on data management and
security.

## Concerns

The lack of pricing information, how the tool works, etc. is a bit of a red
flag. They're trying to make it like a black box, but since you can't know how
black boxes operate, you can't know what the results will be. One
[KiCAD Forum user](https://forum.kicad.info/t/an-automatic-schematic-reviewer/64016/4)
reported that they were excited to follow it, but "they were just using AI where
it works good: to OCR datasheets and fill out investor pitch decks." Again, for
a tool with so many big-time partners, they sure have very little publicity.
