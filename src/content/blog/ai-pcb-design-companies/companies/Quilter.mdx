"Physics-Driven AI that Automates PCB Layout." "Trusted by industry partners:
AWS, Nvidia, and Siemens". Founded in Los Angeles by someone who previously
spent 5 years at SpaceX, with an engineering lead who spent 16 years at Altium.
They've also hired Eric Bogatin, a name that rings a bell with me, since I've
watched his videos with Robert Feranec on various topics in the industry.

## Product

This is a routing engine. You build the schematic in your ECAD, upload it,
select your stackup, constraints, then generate, review, refine, repeat.
Apparently this is a trained neural network too, not just a generative AI.
That's pretty cool.

They have a
[32 minute walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dr6MCJ0mUBc), which
while that's very insightful for someone who wants to get in-depth with the
product, I'm not watching that. Can we please get a 5-minute version of
something like this? It seems like it's just explaining the details of what's in
the pictures anyways.

Pricing is gated behind sales, which I obviously don't like. They say you pay
per project though.

## what they do well

The site looks pretty professional. Blogs, documentation, community, the site
looks like it's designed well and put together, which earns some credibility
points over the obviously-AI-slopped options out there.

## What they suck at

I obviously don't like the pricing strategy. It also seems a bit weird to design
in ECAD, then have to come out of it and upload your board here
[(where they do state that they could take your files)](https://www.quilter.ai/privacy#:~:text=including%20your%20input%20board%20files),
then go back into your ECAD to make finishing touches. I'd really like to see a
plugin or something.

## Online sentiment

[The top comment on this hacker news post is bashing it](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46294254),
complaining aout a host of things and stating that a lot of the routing work
that they're claiming to automate has been automated already.
[One EEVBlog post](https://www.eevblog.com/forum/chat/ai-pcb-layout/) said it
"can't do HDI... microvias... complex breakouts from BGAs"

They also have an
[AMA](https://www.reddit.com/r/ElectricalEngineering/comments/1r7dkwi/our_ai_designed_the_first_autonomously_placed_and/)
where they answer various questions about the app. So at least they're engaging
with the community.
