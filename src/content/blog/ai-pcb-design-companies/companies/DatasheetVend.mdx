DatasheetVend is a product by Gomorra Labs, a Wyoming-based AI company that also
makes an AI assistant for restaurant costs. The domain was registered in March
2026, with the product going live in June of the same year.

## Product

"From a PDF to an answer you can check." The demo on their homepage seems like a
"chat-with-a-PDF" environment, tailored to datasheets. You upload a PDF, it
turns it into structured data for an AI assistant, where you can chat with it.
There's even an AI person, for whatever reason. In their
[component library](https://datasheetvend.com/components), they have 35,000+
datasheets that you can extract. I don't know if they've already been extracted
or if they just have them there for you to extract them, but it's there.

Pricing has a free trial with 5 evaluation passes, then \$3/module (sheesh), a
\$50/month plan with 100 "module credits"/month, optionsl source PDF keep (do
they usually not let you keep the PDFs?), and 50mb uploads. There is also a
professional plan at \$100/month.

Underneath, they tell you what the credits buy you. Some modules (like pinout,
power limits) are 1 credit, while most are 3, and some are 3. And there are
definitely multiple modules per datasheet, so processing a datasheet seems to be
pretty costly.

## What they do well

The pricing is transparent, if a bit high. Easy for people to test out and maybe
give a small commitment to, maybe a full commitment if they enjoy it. They also
have a blog talking about different topics, such as comparint two chips,
strapping pins for MCUs, guides on reading datasheets, and so on.

Quick sidenote, I wonder what these blogs are for. Like, why are they publishing
these? Who is their target audience? Do they expect their customers to be the
