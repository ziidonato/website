Founded in 2018, AllSpice positions themselves as a "Github for ECAD," allowing
clients to store their boards in the cloud where they get Github-like issues,
reviews, branches, etc. DRCY, their AI assistant, can help design and review
boards, referencing the datasheet in its findings.

## Products

### AllSpice Hub

[AllSpice Hub](https://hub.allspice.io/explore/repos) is their main product, a
"Github for ECAD."
[It's based on a fork of Gitea](https://allspice.io/post/which-git-clients-work-for-hardware-engineers-and-why#:~:text=Allspice%20Hub%20is%20based%20on%20a%20fork%20of%20Gitea),
and comes with a host of Github-like features. You can
[update boards using commits](https://hub.allspice.io/MUREX/electrical/commit/7fcfe4ee71e1514080ec1bc95f309e7db78b61ad#diff-eed014d32bb0f702e9dcb329d72e79db7781db0b),
open and close issues, view schematics and boards in different formats (like
[KiCAD](https://hub.allspice.io/MUREX/electrical) and
[Altium](https://hub.allspice.io/Actions-Demos/Action-digikey-search-html-report-Demo)),
use releases, etc. I didn't want to make an account, but
[DRCY](https://hub.allspice.io/Actions-Demos/Action-digikey-search-html-report-Demo/drcy),
their second product, is here as well.

If you look at their pricing, it's a good clip. There are 4 plans, and the first
one starts a \$34,800 per year. This is clearly marketed as an enterprise
product.

## What they do well

They seem to have a pretty good strategy for acquiring/processing the
datasheets. In their
[paper documenting how the agent works](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15672v1),
they have a system that can query Digikey's API, files uploaded by clients, and
other sources to retrieve the datasheets, given an MPN.

I think the ability to see diffs on the board is pretty cool. I don't know how
much I'd pay for that, but I think it's pretty cool.

## What they suck at

It doesn't tell you where in the datasheet it got its source from. No highlight,
page number, section, anything. They also don't tell you the accuracy of their
reviews or their data extraction pipelines. Since there is no real sense of
determinism, I can't imagine the accuracy is very high.

## Online sentiment

[People don't seem to see the benefit to it versus using git.](https://www.reddit.com/r/ECE/comments/1d9rn4a/allspice/)
Git already works with ECAD software (I can attest, I have a KiCAD board in a
github repo right now), so that functionality wouldn't be worth the
enterprise-grade pricing for them.
[According to one user who was tasked with testing these sorts of applications](https://www.reddit.com/r/PCB/s/kDVf8C3dzq),
DRCY was "far from accurate", but reviewing the schatic on every commit is
pretty cool.
