Silixon is a small European company with the goal to take you "from an idea to a
real circuit board".

## Product

They have one product, which is "idea to PCB". Their front page shows a chatbot
(with a link to setup an MCP) that asks you questions to clarify your intent,
generates the board, and then checks the board before you send it to get
manufactured. They advertise that it's for people of all expertise-levels,
beginners who want to build but dont know anything about electronics, to
hobbyists who need a first-board iteration quickly.

There are example boards where you can click around their environment, which I
like. You can do everything except edit the board and chat with the AI. While I
understand the chat restriction, I think allowing local edits that aren't saved
wouldn't be too hard.

Pricing is free for one project every 2 weeks, while pro is ~\$15/month (they
use euros) for unlimited projects, community access, remixes, private PCBS, and
KiCad import/export. You can pay ~\$100 for yearly access as well.

## What they do well

Gotta love the pricing. Transparent, accessible, no enterprise nonsense. They're
kneecapping themselves from the enterprise market that way, but one could say
they're optimizing for their likely audience.

It's also a complete platform, building both the schematic and the PCB, which
some of the other offerings here don't do.

[They entered a PCB-Golf contest and got 3rd place](https://silixon.io/blog/pcbgolf),
so while that's not a benchmark, it's at least a bit of a testament to their
quality.

## What they suck at

They're trying to market by making blog posts and comparison pages, etc., but it
doesn't seem to be working. Nobody's really talking about them except for
themselves. I know they have sponsored at least one video, so they're definitely
branching out and doing the traditional "pay for ads" marketing, which I believe
is the best option to actually get customers.
